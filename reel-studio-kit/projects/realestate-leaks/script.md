# realestate-leaks — "4 galtiyan jo property leads ko leak karti hain"

Format: dark "leaks -> fixes" explainer with voice, background music and a sound on every animation.
Inspired by the format of a gym-website explainer; own words, own visuals (houses, map pins, site
visits, brochure), own palette (navy + gold + coral), own labels (GALTI / HAL).

Hook: 5,000 views -> 2 site visits -> "baaki kahan gaye?"
Galti 1 PROFILE: only a phone number, no price/location -> "SKIP" stamp
Galti 2 CALL: you were on site, call missed -> another broker picks up
Galti 3 VISIT: no reminder, no map pin -> "NO SHOW" stamp
Galti 4 FOLLOW-UP: brochure sent, then silence -> "DEAL GAYI" stamp
Turn: "Ab yahi sab, ek website pe" (impact)
Hal 1 listings with price, location, photos, video
Hal 2 enquiry form -> lead on WhatsApp
Hal 3 site-visit slot booking with map pin + reminder
Hal 4 all enquiries in one sheet, follow-ups visible
CTA: comment PROPERTY

Honesty notes: no price promised for this feature set; "reminder" = calendar booking page reminders;
"seedha WhatsApp pe" = form that opens / sends to WhatsApp. Confirm these are in your package.
Voice: George (premade, free, male narrator).
Music: "Hip Hop 02" by Lily J (Mixkit Stock Music Free License). SFX: Mixkit Sound Effects Free License.
