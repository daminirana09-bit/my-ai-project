# website-zaroori — "Website nahi hai? ₹5,000 mein banwao"

Goal: businesses without a website DM the page owner to get one made for ₹5,000.
Voice speaks as "hum" (we), so it fits any creator.

## Hook (max 3 s)
"Website nahi hai? Toh customer gaya."
Screen: Title "Website nahi = customer gaya" + sticker "✕ no website".

## Beats
1. "Ek survey mein 39% logon ne kaha — website nahi thi, toh kharida hi nahi." Screen: BigNumber 39% count-up, small line "US survey, 2025".
2. "Aur 45% ko aisa business asli hi nahi lagta." Screen: BigNumber 45% + sticker "asli?".
3. "Customer pehle Google karta hai — wahan aap nahi, toh competitor." Screen: search card, "Aapki dukaan ✕ / Competitor ✓".

## Twist
"Aur website banwana mehenga nahi hai."
Screen: big "₹50,000?" crossed out.

## Offer
"Hum aapke business ki website banayenge — sirf ₹5,000 mein."
Screen: BigNumber ₹5,000 + sticker "sirf".
"Mobile pe perfect, WhatsApp button, Google Map location, aur aapke rate."
Screen: Card with 4 ticks popping on each word.

## CTA (comment keyword)
"Comment karo WEBSITE — hum DM karenge."
EndCard keyword WEBSITE. What the user sends: a DM with the offer details.

## Facts and sources
- 39% declined to visit or buy from a business because it lacked a website; 45% say businesses without websites feel less "real". DreamHost 2026 Local Business Trust Index, 1,201 US adults, Nov 2025: https://www.dreamhost.com/blog/?p=78494
  - **US** survey: labelled "US survey, 2025" on screen.
- "₹50,000?" in the twist is shown as a question (a common assumption), not a claim about market prices.
- The ₹5,000 offer and what it includes are the page owner's own offer — confirm before posting.

## Spoken script
voice.txt (Devanagari, for the voice) and captions.txt (Hinglish, on screen): same words, same order.
