# website-zaroori — "Website har business ke liye kyun zaroori hai"

## Hook (max 3 s)
"Website nahi hai? Toh 10 mein se 4 customer gaye."
Screen: big number "4/10" + title "Website nahi = customer gaya".

## Beats
1. "Ek survey mein 39% logon ne kaha — website nahi thi, toh kharida hi nahi." Screen: BigNumber 39% count-up + small source line.
2. "Aur 45% ko aisa business asli hi nahi lagta." Screen: BigNumber 45% + sticker "asli?"
3. "Aaj customer pehle Google karta hai — timing, rate, reviews." Screen: search card with 3 lines popping on each word.
4. "Wahan aap nahi dikhe, toh competitor dikhega." Screen: card "Aapki dukaan ✕ / Competitor ✓".

## Twist
"Aur sabse badi baat — Instagram page aapka nahi hai. Account band, ya reach giri — sab gaya. Website aapki apni dukaan hai, internet pe."
Screen: title "Instagram = kiraye ka ghar", then "Website = apni zameen".

## CTA (comment keyword)
"Shuru karne ke liye bas ek page chahiye — kaam, rate, location aur WhatsApp button. Comment karo WEBSITE — poori checklist aapko mil jayegi."
Screen: card with 4 ticks, then EndCard keyword WEBSITE.
What the user sends: `checklist.md` in this folder (one-page website checklist).

## Facts and sources
- 39% declined to visit or buy from a business because it lacked a website; 45% say businesses without websites feel less "real". DreamHost 2026 Local Business Trust Index, 1,201 US adults, Nov 2025: https://www.dreamhost.com/blog/?p=78494
  - Note: this is a **US** survey. On screen it is labelled "US survey, 2025". "10 mein se 4" is the 39% rounded.
- Not used: "63% of Indian small businesses have no website" (GoDaddy/RedShift) — it is from 2015, too old: https://www.dnaindia.com/technology/report-nearly-2-out-of-3-small-business-in-the-country-do-not-have-a-web-presence-godaddy-survey-2127625/amp
- "Customer pehle Google karta hai", "Instagram page aapka nahi hai" — opinion / common sense, no number claimed.
