# Google Business Profile — Free Setup Guide (by @solvix_web)

Google Search aur Maps pe apna business free mein laane ke steps.
(Google ki official help: https://support.google.com/business/answer/10514137)

## Step 1 — Profile banao ya claim karo
1. Ek Google account se login karo (business wala email ho toh behtar).
2. **business.google.com/create** kholo aur business ka naam daalo.
3. Agar aapka business pehle se Google pe dikh raha hai, toh naya mat banao — use **claim / request ownership** karo.

## Step 2 — Details bharo
4. Sahi **category** chuno (jaise Cafe, Dentist, Real estate agency).
5. Customers aapki jagah aate hain toh **address** daalo; aap unke paas jaate ho toh **service area**.
6. **Phone number** aur **website** daalo.
   - Naam mein extra keywords mat bharo (jaise "Best Cafe in Jaipur") — ye Google ke rules ke khilaaf hai.

## Step 3 — Verify karo
7. Google jo tarika de (phone/SMS, email, video, ya kabhi postcard), usse verify karo.
8. Verify hone ke baad hi aapka business Search aur Maps pe dikhta hai.

## Verify ke baad (ye mat bhoolna)
- **Timing** (holiday timing bhi), **photos** (bahar, andar, kaam/khaana), aur **description** daalo.
- Har review ka jawab do — achhe ka bhi, bure ka bhi.
- Har hafte ek **update/offer post** karo.

Google profile se log aapko dhoondh lete hain. Website pe woh poori jaankari dekh ke decide karte hain —
