Steps (from Google's help page "Add or claim your Business Profile",
https://support.google.com/business/answer/10514137):
1. business.google.com/create, enter the business name (if it already exists: request ownership / claim).
2. Category, address/service area, phone, website.
3. Verify (Google chooses the method). Customers can find the profile only after verification.
Free: no fee to create the listing (third-party guides; Google's page doesn't mention a charge).
CTA: comment GOOGLE -> setup guide (projects/google-profile/guide.md).
