# Hotel / Homestay Website Checklist — by @solvix_web

Ye 10 cheezein aapke hotel ki website pe honi chahiye:

1. **Yaad rehne wala domain** — property ka naam + .in / .com (jaise pineviewstay.in)
2. **Upar ek bada photo** — view ya property ka best shot, aur ek line: kahan ho, kya khaas hai
3. **Har room — photos, price aur kya-kya milta hai** (bed, view, heater, breakfast)
4. **Google Map location** — aur station / airport / bus stand se kaise pahunchein
5. **Check-in / check-out timing aur rules** (ID, pets, cancellation)
6. **Google reviews** — rating aur 2–3 asli reviews
7. **"Room book karo" WhatsApp button** — dates aur guests ke saath seedha message
8. **Photos** — rooms, bathroom, view, dining, parking, aas-paas ki jagah
9. **Packages / season offers** — honeymoon, family, long stay
10. **Instagram bio mein website ka link** — aur website pe Instagram ka
