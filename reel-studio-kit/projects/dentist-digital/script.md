# dentist-digital — "Dentist ho? Patient aapko Google pe dhoondh raha hai"

Goal: dentists comment DENTIST to get a free online check-up of their clinic from @solvix_web.
Style: story hook (night toothache -> Google), same cream story look as website-zaroori.

## Hook (max 3 s)
"Raat ko daant mein dard. Patient ne pehle kya kiya?"
Screen: phone lock screen 2:14 AM, then search bar.

## Beats
1. "Google kiya — dentist near me." Screen: search types "dentist near me".
2. "Teen clinic dikhe — do ki website thi." Screen: 3 result cards, two with website / timing / map.
3. "Aapka clinic? Sirf ek naam." Screen: third card greyed out, "sirf naam".
4. "Ek US survey mein 84% patients ne kaha — doctor chunne se pehle woh online reviews padhte hain." Screen: ring 84%, source line.
5. "Aur Delhi-NCR mein 78% patients doctor se milne ke baad bhi Google karte hain." Screen: ring 78%, source line.
6. "Matlab patient online hai. Sawaal ye hai — aap hain?" Screen: title with serif "aap".

## Twist
"Par dhyaan rahe — National Dental Commission ke naye rules mein testimonial aur before-after photos mana hain."
Screen: PROBLEM-style badge "RULES", card with ✕ Testimonials ✕ Before/after ✕ Jhoothe daave.

## Offer
"Isliye hum dentists ke liye rules ke andar digital setup banate hain — website, Google profile, appointment button, aur patient education posts."
Screen: phone builds a clinic website, checklist ticks on each item.

## CTA (comment keyword)
"Comment karo DENTIST — hum aapke clinic ka free online check-up karenge."
What the user sends: a short free audit of the clinic's Google profile / website / Instagram (confirm you can do this).

## Facts and sources
- 84% of patients check online reviews before choosing a provider; 51% read 6+ reviews. rater8, 1,000+ US patients, reported Feb 18, 2025 (survey fielded Dec 2024):
  https://www.medicaleconomics.com/view/patients-trust-online-reviews-but-they-don-t-leave-them
  - US survey, healthcare in general (not dentists only). On screen: "US survey · rater8".
- 78.5% of 1,000 Delhi-NCR patients searched Google/social media AFTER doctor visits (lack of clarity on diagnosis/next steps). India Patient Navigation and Confusion Index 2026 (Pacific OneHealth Hospital + IMAPH), PTI, Jun 9, 2026:
  https://theprint.in/india/nearly-80-pc-delhi-ncr-patients-turn-to-google-after-doctor-visits-study/2955453/
  - Said as "doctor se milne ke baad bhi", not "before choosing".
- National Dental Commission advisory, Oct 14, 2026: cautions against recording/broadcasting procedures, patient testimonials, before-after photos, misleading claims, self-promotional publicity (Clauses 8.1–8.3, Revised Dentists (Code of Ethics) Regulations 2014):
  https://medicaldialogues.in/news/health/doctors/national-dental-commission-warns-dentists-against-unethical-social-media-advertising-181335
  - The article does not say what is allowed. The offer is framed as information (website, Google profile, booking, education), not ads or testimonials. Not legal advice.
- Not used: dental-only review stats (2017 Ceatus, undated Dandy, unsourced agency blogs).
