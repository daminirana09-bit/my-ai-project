# dentist-digital — "Doctor sahab, naye patient kam kyun?"

Goal: dentists comment DENTIST to get a free online check-up of their clinic from @solvix_web.
Speaks directly to the dentist (sales pitch). Same cream story look as website-zaroori.

## Hook (max 3 s)
"Doctor sahab, kaam aapka best hai — par naye patient kam kyun?"
Screen: empty dental chair + title "Chair khaali kyun?" (serif accent).

## Beats
1. "Kyunki aaj patient pehle Google karta hai — dentist near me." Screen: phone search types "dentist near me".
2. "Ek US survey mein 84% patients ne kaha — doctor chunne se pehle woh online reviews padhte hain." Screen: ring 84%, "US survey · rater8".
3. "Wahan aapka clinic nahi dikha, toh patient doosre clinic chala gaya." Screen: results — your clinic "Koi result nahi", other clinic ✓; patient walks to the other clinic.

## Twist
"Aur galat promotion kiya, toh National Dental Commission action le sakta hai."
Screen: badge "RULES", card ✕ Testimonials ✕ Before/after ✕ Jhoothe daave.

## Offer
"Isliye hum dentists ke liye rules ke andar digital setup banate hain — website, Google profile, online appointment, aur patient education posts."
Screen: phone builds a clinic website; checklist ticks on each item.
"Aap patient sambhaliye, online hum sambhalenge." Screen: split card "Aap: patient" / "Hum: online".

## CTA (comment keyword)
"Comment karo DENTIST — aapke clinic ka free online check-up hum karenge."
What the user sends: a short free audit of the clinic's Google profile / website / Instagram.

## Facts and sources
- 84% of patients check online reviews before choosing a provider; 51% read 6+ reviews. rater8, 1,000+ US patients, reported Feb 18, 2025 (survey fielded Dec 2024):
  https://www.medicaleconomics.com/view/patients-trust-online-reviews-but-they-don-t-leave-them
  - US survey, healthcare in general (not dentists only). On screen: "US survey · rater8".
- (Not used in this version) 78.5% of 1,000 Delhi-NCR patients searched Google/social media AFTER doctor visits (lack of clarity on diagnosis/next steps). India Patient Navigation and Confusion Index 2026 (Pacific OneHealth Hospital + IMAPH), PTI, Jun 9, 2026:
  https://theprint.in/india/nearly-80-pc-delhi-ncr-patients-turn-to-google-after-doctor-visits-study/2955453/
  - Said as "doctor se milne ke baad bhi", not "before choosing".
- "action le sakta hai": the advisory says any established violation "may invite appropriate disciplinary action" (show-cause, suspension, removal from register).
- National Dental Commission advisory, Oct 14, 2026: cautions against recording/broadcasting procedures, patient testimonials, before-after photos, misleading claims, self-promotional publicity (Clauses 8.1–8.3, Revised Dentists (Code of Ethics) Regulations 2014):
  https://medicaldialogues.in/news/health/doctors/national-dental-commission-warns-dentists-against-unethical-social-media-advertising-181335
  - The article does not say what is allowed. The offer is framed as information (website, Google profile, booking, education), not ads or testimonials. Not legal advice.
- Not used: dental-only review stats (2017 Ceatus, undated Dandy, unsourced agency blogs).
