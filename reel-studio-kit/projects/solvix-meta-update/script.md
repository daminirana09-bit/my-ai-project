# solvix-meta-update — Meta's new brand ad formats, explained for Solvix's page

Format idea from the reference in the user's Drive folder (creator explaining on a laptop with a face bubble);
own script, own visuals: Riya (illustrated Solvix presenter, female voice Sarah) in a bubble, laptop page with three
phone mock-ups, a pointing hand, Solvix logo colours (black + neon green). No Meta logos or pages are copied.

Facts (Meta Brand Building Summit, 15-16 Oct 2026) and where they come from:
- First Impression Ads: first ad slot in Feed, one brand per day, early test in the US only.
- Reels Trending Ads: ad right after the most-watched Reels on Facebook and Instagram; open to all advertisers,
  15 always-on topics.
- Ads Super Burst: book a 3-24 hour window for TV-like reach, optional category exclusivity, planned in Reach
  Planner, general availability rolling out in phases.
