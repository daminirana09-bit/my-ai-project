# Cafe Website Checklist — by @solvix_web

Ye 10 cheezein aapke cafe ki website pe honi chahiye:

1. **Yaad rehne wala domain** — cafe ka naam + .in / .com (jaise kulhadcorner.in)
2. **Upar ek achhi photo** — aapki best dish ya cafe ka mahaul, aur ek line: aap kya ho, kahan ho
3. **Menu, price ke saath** — log DM mein "rate kya hai?" poochhna band karenge
4. **Google Map location** — ek click mein raasta
5. **Timing** — kab khulte ho, kab band (weekend/holiday alag ho to woh bhi)
6. **Google reviews** — rating aur 2–3 asli reviews
7. **"Table book karo" WhatsApp button** — har page pe, phone pe neeche chipka hua
8. **Photos** — khaana, seating, outdoor/indoor, parking
9. **Offers / events** — weekend special, live music, birthday packages
10. **Instagram bio mein website ka link** — aur website pe Instagram ka
